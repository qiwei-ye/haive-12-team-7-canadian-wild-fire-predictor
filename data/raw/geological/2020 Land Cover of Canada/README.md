**File name:** `landcover-2020-classification.tif`

**File source:** [Natural Resources Canada - 2020 Land Cover of Canada](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/ee1580ab-a23d-4f86-a09b-79763677eb47); [Natural Resources Canada - Land Cover Class Index](https://datacube-prod-data-public.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/store/land/landcover/ClassIndex_v2.pdf).

**File purpose:** Provide Canada's 2020 land-cover classes as a single-band categorical raster.

**Time horizon:** static

**Grid resolution:** 30 metres x 30 metres (native projected grid)

| Variable | Definition | Unit/format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Band 1 (`Canada2020`) | Land-cover class assigned to each pixel. | Unsigned 8-bit categorical code; 0 = No data; 1 = Temperate or sub-polar needleleaf forest; 2 = Sub-polar taiga needleleaf forest; 5 = Temperate or sub-polar broadleaf deciduous forest; 6 = Mixed forest; 8 = Temperate or sub-polar shrubland; 10 = Temperate or sub-polar grassland; 11 = Sub-polar or polar shrubland-lichen-moss; 12 = Sub-polar or polar grassland-lichen-moss; 13 = Sub-polar or polar barren-lichen-moss; 14 = Wetland; 15 = Cropland; 16 = Barren land; 17 = Urban and built-up; 18 = Water; 19 = Snow and ice |
